Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 14, 2026

Syno, Inc. ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Syno mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your personal information when you use our App and related services. By using the App, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

When you use the App, you may provide us with:

  • account registration information such as your name, email address, and password;
  • voice recordings and audio input captured through the App's voice interface;
  • text input, including tasks, notes, reminders, and other content you create;
  • calendar events, scheduling preferences, and time-related data;
  • health and wellness information you voluntarily input, such as medication reminders, fitness goals, or lifestyle preferences; and
  • feedback, support requests, and communications you send to us.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you use the App, we may automatically collect:

  • device information such as device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and push notification tokens;
  • usage data including features accessed, interactions with the App, timestamps, and session duration;
  • crash reports and performance logs; and
  • approximate location data (if you grant permission) for location-relevant features such as weather or local recommendations.

1.3 Information from Third-Party Services

If you connect third-party services (e.g., calendar providers) to the App, we may receive data from those services in accordance with their terms and your permissions.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • provide, maintain, and improve the App and its features;
  • process your voice and text inputs through AI models to generate structured outputs such as tasks, reminders, calendar events, and recommendations;
  • personalize your experience and provide context-aware suggestions;
  • send you notifications, reminders, and alerts you have configured;
  • respond to your support requests and communications;
  • analyze usage patterns to improve the App's functionality and user experience;
  • detect, prevent, and address technical issues and security threats; and
  • comply with legal obligations.

3. Voice Data Processing

Voice is central to the App's functionality. When you use voice input:

  • your audio is transmitted to our servers and/or third-party AI providers for transcription and processing;
  • transcribed text is further processed by AI models to extract actionable information;
  • we may retain transcriptions to provide features like memory recall and context continuity; and
  • raw audio recordings may be temporarily stored for processing and are deleted after transcription is complete, unless you explicitly choose to save them.

We do not use your voice recordings to train general-purpose AI models. Your voice data is used solely to provide the Service to you.

4. AI Processing, Limitations, and Third-Party Providers

The App uses artificial intelligence to process your inputs and generate outputs such as tasks, reminders, calendar events, and suggestions.

AI Limitations and Errors

Syno's AI features are provided on an "as is" basis and may produce inaccurate, incomplete, or unexpected results. Specifically:

  • AI-generated transcriptions may contain errors or misinterpret spoken input;
  • tasks, reminders, events, and other structured outputs generated by AI may be incorrectly categorized, timed, or formatted;
  • context-aware suggestions and recommendations may not be accurate or appropriate for your situation;
  • AI models may misunderstand ambiguous or complex inputs; and
  • AI outputs should be reviewed by you before relying on them for any important decision or action.

You should not rely solely on Syno's AI-generated outputs for health, medical, legal, financial, or other critical decisions. We are not responsible for any consequences arising from errors, omissions, or inaccuracies in AI-generated content.

Third-Party AI Providers

The App uses third-party AI services (such as OpenAI) to process your inputs. When your data is sent to these providers:

  • it is transmitted securely and used solely for the purpose of generating a response to your request;
  • we rely on the data processing agreements and privacy commitments of these providers; and
  • these providers may have their own data retention and processing policies, which we encourage you to review.

We select providers who commit to not using customer input data for model training, but we cannot guarantee the practices of third parties. A current list of sub-processors is available upon request.

5. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information with:

  • AI and cloud service providers who process data on our behalf to operate the App;
  • analytics providers who help us understand App usage (in aggregated or de-identified form);
  • professional advisors such as lawyers, auditors, and accountants as necessary;
  • law enforcement or government authorities when required by law, legal process, or to protect our rights, safety, or property; and
  • a successor entity in the event of a merger, acquisition, bankruptcy, or sale of assets, in which case you will be notified.

6. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure cloud infrastructure. We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest, access controls, and regular security assessments. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your data may be processed and stored in the United States or other jurisdictions where our service providers operate. By using the App, you consent to the transfer of your data to these jurisdictions.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. Specifically:

  • account information is retained until you delete your account;
  • User Content (tasks, memories, notes, etc.) is retained until you delete individual items or your account;
  • voice transcriptions are retained as part of your User Content;
  • raw audio recordings are deleted after processing unless explicitly saved;
  • usage logs and analytics data are retained in de-identified form for up to 24 months; and
  • certain data may be retained longer if required by law or for legitimate business purposes (e.g., fraud prevention, legal disputes).

  • access the personal information we hold about you;
  • request correction of inaccurate information;
  • request deletion of your personal information;
  • object to or restrict certain processing of your data;

  • withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent.
